About ClearPath Safety Hub

Knowledge that makes communities safer.

ClearPath Safety Hub is a public-education project. We turn carefully reviewed research on crime, victimisation and justice into clear, practical guidance that anyone can use — and we link every claim back to its source.

Our mission

Help people understand, prevent, and respond to crime.

Crime data is everywhere, but trustworthy plain-language guidance is not. We close that gap.

We take research on crime, victimisation and justice, have it reviewed by people with subject expertise, and turn it into explanations, teaching material and practical advice — always with the source attached.

Explain risk and response

Who this is for

For everyone looking for reliable context.

Students, teachers, community groups, parents, journalists, and anyone who wants reliable, non-alarmist information about crime and justice.

Our approach

Values that guide every page.

Evidence first

Clarity

Transparency

Practicality

Why we exist

Most people encounter crime statistics or headlines without context or practical next steps. ClearPath Safety Hub exists to turn that noise into usable knowledge so communities can make better decisions about safety, prevention and response.
