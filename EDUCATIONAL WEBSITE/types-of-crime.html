Pattern of Crime

Understand the different forms crime takes.

A plain-language overview of common offence types — what they involve, the warning signs, and how they’re usually reported. These are educational summaries, not legal definitions.

i

A note on definitions

Legal definitions and penalties vary by country and change over time. Use this page as a starting point, then check your local law for specifics.

Violent

Assault

A threat or attempt that creates a reasonable expectation of imminent harmful or offensive contact.

Report to police
Violent

Robbery

The unlawful taking of property from a person through force or the threat of immediate force.

Report to police
Violent

Domestic & family violence

Abuse within a household or intimate relationship — physical, emotional, financial or coercive control.

Report to police or a support service
Property

Burglary

Entering a building without permission in order to steal or commit another offence.

Report to police
Property

Vehicle crime

Theft of a vehicle, theft from a vehicle, or taking one without the owner’s consent.

Report to police
Property

Vandalism & criminal damage

Deliberately damaging or destroying property, from graffiti to arson.

Report to police
Financial

Scam & phishing

Deceptive schemes and fake messages designed to steal money, personal details, or account access.

Report to your bank & fraud line
Financial

Identity theft

Using someone’s personal information to impersonate them, usually for financial gain.

Report to police & fraud line
Financial

Investment fraud

Schemes that promise high, low-risk returns but take your money instead.

Report to your regulator
Cyber

Hacking & malware

Gaining unauthorised access to devices or networks, often through malicious software.

Report to police or CERT
Cyber

Online harassment & stalking

Persistent abuse, threats or monitoring carried out through digital platforms.

Report to the platform & police
Cyber

Sextortion & image abuse

Blackmail using intimate images, or sharing them without consent.

Report to police & the platform
Public order

Drug offences

Possession, supply or production of controlled substances.

Report to police
Public order

Hate crime

Offences motivated by hostility toward identity — race, religion, disability, sexuality or gender.

Report to police
Public order

Weapons offences

Carrying, using or possessing prohibited weapons, including knives and firearms.

Report to police
